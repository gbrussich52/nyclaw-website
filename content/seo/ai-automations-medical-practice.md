# 5 AI Automations Every NYC Medical Practice Should Be Running

The average NYC medical practice loses $30,000–$80,000 per year to a combination of no-shows, lapsed patients, poor review volume, and administrative time that could be automated. None of that is fixed by hiring more front desk staff. It's fixed by changing the systems.

Here are five AI automations that practices across specialties — primary care, dermatology, orthopedics, psychiatry — are running today.

## 1. No-Show Reduction Sequences

The national average no-show rate for medical practices is 15–20%. In NYC, where patients have more options and are generally more time-constrained, it skews higher.

A single reminder the day before doesn't move the needle. What works is a multi-touch sequence:

- **5 days before**: Appointment confirmation with easy reschedule link
- **48 hours before**: Reminder with intake forms if they haven't completed them, plus parking/transit info
- **Morning of**: Final reminder with your address and a direct line to call if something comes up

Each message is personalized and goes out automatically. No staff involvement after the initial setup. Practices using structured reminder sequences report no-show rate drops of 25–50%. At $150–400 per missed appointment slot, the math is significant.

## 2. Intake Form Automation

Paper intake forms are a 20-minute wait room experience that frustrates patients and creates a transcription burden for staff. Digital forms sent before the visit solve both problems.

An AI agent can be configured to automatically send intake forms when an appointment is booked, follow up if they're not completed, and route completed forms into your EMR or a simple document folder. Patients arrive ready. Staff skip the clipboard handoff.

For practices doing 30+ appointments per day, this alone can recover 45–90 minutes of administrative time daily.

## 3. Lapsed Patient Reactivation

Every practice has a population of patients who haven't been in for 12–24+ months. Chronic condition patients who went quiet. Annual check-up patients who forgot. People who moved and came back.

Most practices do nothing with this list because running a reactivation campaign manually takes time they don't have. An AI campaign handles it: identify overdue patients, send a personalized outreach ("It's been a while — you're due for your annual wellness visit"), include a direct booking link, and follow up once if they don't respond.

A well-run reactivation campaign converts 8–15% of dormant patients. For a practice with 400 lapsed patients, that's 32–60 reactivated appointments — without spending a dollar on new patient acquisition.

## 4. Post-Visit Follow-Up and Care Compliance

After a procedure, a new prescription, or a significant diagnosis, patients often have questions they didn't ask in the room. They also frequently don't follow through on referrals, labs, or follow-up appointments.

An AI can handle the follow-up layer: a check-in 48 hours post-visit ("How are you feeling after your procedure yesterday?"), a nudge if a referral or lab order hasn't been fulfilled within 10 days, and a reminder when a follow-up appointment is due. Automated, personalized, and documented.

This improves outcomes and reduces the liability exposure that comes from patients who slip through the cracks on care plans.

## 5. Google Review Generation

Medical practices live and die by their online reputation. A dermatology practice with 12 Google reviews loses patients to a competitor with 200 — regardless of actual quality of care.

The problem isn't that patients don't want to leave reviews. It's that they forget, and no one asks at the right moment. An AI sends a review request automatically after a positive appointment signal — a patient who confirmed, showed up, and didn't flag any issues. Short message, direct Google Business link, sent within 24 hours of the visit.

Practices using automated review requests see 3–5× more monthly reviews compared to manual or no solicitation. For high-competition specialties in NYC, that volume can be the difference between showing up on page one or being invisible.

## The Bigger Picture

None of these automations require replacing your EMR or hiring an AI consultant on retainer. They're systems that run in the background, handle the follow-up layer, and free your staff to focus on actual patient care.

The practices seeing the biggest impact aren't the ones with the biggest budgets — they're the ones who got the systems right.
