# 5 AI Automations Every Dental Practice Should Be Running

Most dental practices run the same manual processes they ran ten years ago. Appointment reminders go out via a staff member making calls. Review requests get sent inconsistently, if at all. New patient follow-ups fall through the cracks between the front desk and the treatment coordinator.

None of that requires a human. All of it is costing you money.

Here are five AI automations that dental practices are running today — none of which require replacing your existing software, adding headcount, or doing anything more complicated than a one-time setup.

## 1. Appointment Reminder Sequences (That Actually Reduce No-Shows)

The standard: one reminder the day before. The problem: one reminder isn't enough, and it's often too late.

A well-configured AI reminder sequence looks like this:
- **7 days out**: Friendly confirmation + link to reschedule if needed
- **48 hours out**: Reminder with parking/directions and what to bring
- **Day of**: Final nudge with your address + a phone number to call if they can't make it

Each message is personalized — the patient's name, the appointment type, the time. It goes out automatically via SMS or email, whichever they prefer. No staff time. No dropped balls.

The data on this is consistent: multi-touch reminder sequences reduce no-shows by 30–50% compared to single reminders. At $150–400 per missed appointment, that adds up fast.

## 2. Reactivation Campaigns for Overdue Patients

Every practice has a list of patients who haven't been in for 18+ months. Most practices do nothing with that list.

An AI reactivation campaign does the math for you: identify who's overdue based on your records, send a warm outreach message, and book them back in. Not a mass blast — a personalized message that references their last visit, reminds them it's time for a cleaning, and makes it easy to schedule.

Done right, reactivation campaigns convert 10–20% of dormant patients. For a practice with 300 overdue patients, that's 30–60 new appointments without spending a dollar on ads.

## 3. Post-Treatment Follow-Up

After a filling, an extraction, or any procedure with recovery, patients appreciate a check-in. They're also more likely to come back if they feel cared for.

An AI can send a follow-up message 24–48 hours post-treatment: "How are you feeling after yesterday's procedure? Let us know if you have any questions — and if anything doesn't feel right, don't hesitate to call." Simple. Human. Automatic.

This serves two purposes: it's genuine patient care, and it surfaces problems early before they become complaints or refund requests. A patient who gets a check-in and responds "actually, I'm still in a lot of pain" can be triaged immediately — before they leave a bad review because they felt ignored.

## 4. Review Request Automation

Google reviews drive new patient acquisition more than almost anything else. Most dental practices get reviews inconsistently because asking for them manually feels awkward and gets deprioritized.

An AI review request goes out automatically after a positive appointment signal — a patient who confirmed their appointment, showed up, and didn't report any issues. The message is short, personalized, and includes a direct link to your Google Business profile.

The timing matters: requests sent within 2 hours of a positive appointment get 3–5x higher conversion than those sent days later. Automated means every eligible patient gets asked, every time, at the right moment.

## 5. New Patient Intake Before the First Visit

Paper forms in the waiting room are a 1990s solution. New patients filling out intake on their phone the night before — with an AI that follows up if the form isn't submitted — is what modern practices run.

An AI intake flow sends a link to your intake forms 48 hours before a new patient's first appointment. If they don't complete it within 24 hours, it sends a reminder. If they have a question, it answers common intake questions automatically. By the time they walk in the door, their chart is ready, chair time isn't eaten up by paperwork, and the first impression is competent and organized.

## The Common Thread

None of these automations are complicated. None of them require a 6-month software implementation. They work through the channels your patients already use — text message, email — and they run in the background without your staff doing anything.

The practices that run these automations consistently see two things: higher revenue per patient (from fewer no-shows, more reactivations, more referrals driven by reviews) and less front desk stress (because the routine follow-up work is handled).

You didn't become a dentist to chase appointment confirmations. AI handles the chase so you can focus on the patients who show up.
