6. **`/blog/agentic-ai-security-best-practices` missing FAQ section and FAQJsonLd** — Same issue. 9. **`/blog/revenuecat-pulse` missing FAQ section and FAQJsonLd** — RevenueCat take-home assignment post has no FAQ or structured data beyond ArticleJsonLd now added. Low-priority fix given the post's niche developer audience, but worth adding for completeness.

### Medium Priority

10. **Zero keyword ranking visibility** — nyclaw.io did not appear in the top visible results for any of the 5 tracked keywords. Competitors (HummingAgent, WestchesterAI, aiagencyny.com) have dedicated geographic landing pages with exact-match domain advantages or deep location-specific content. The locations pages are thin on supporting blog content. **Fix:** Prioritize `/blog/ai-appointment-scheduling` and `/blog/ai-agent-vs-virtual-assistant` from the HIGH queue, and ensure each links back to the location pages.

Competitor pages use titles like "AI Consulting Westchester County NY" for geographic SEO. **Fix:** Consider updating to include "Westchester & NYC" in the title, and add a `LocalBusiness` or `Service` JSON-LD schema with service area markup.
