**Root cause:** Content volume and geo-specificity gap vs. competitors. HummingAgent has a dedicated `/locations/new-york/westchester-county` page optimized for "AI automation westchester." aiagencyny.com has a `/best-ai-agencies-new-york-city-ny` roundup page that ranks for NYC queries.
**Recommended fix:**
- Ship the remaining HIGH-priority content calendar articles (see Content Status section)

### 2. Content calendar significantly out of date (MEDIUM)

At least 10 blog posts have been shipped since then that are not tracked in the calendar: `ai-automation-cost-westchester`, `chatgpt-vs-ai-consultant`, `ai-automations-car-dealerships`, `ai-automations-dental`, `ai-automations-medical`, `ai-automations-plumbing-hvac`, `ai-law-firm-client-intake`, `zapier-vs-ai-agent`, `agentic-ai-security-best-practices`, `revenuecat-pulse`.
**Recommended fix:** This run added the 3 most notable missing posts to the Published table. The full calendar needs a complete reconciliation pass.

### 3. Sitemap `lastModified` dates are stale for several pages (LOW)
**Finding:** `/blog/ai-automations-dental` shows `lastModified: '2026-07-28'` in the sitemap, but the file's `dateModified` is `2026-06-16` and the sitemap date predates it. Multiple blog posts show `2026-07-28` as a blanket date that was likely set during a mass-update rather than reflecting the actual last content change.
**Recommended fix:** Audit each entry's lastModified against the actual file's `datePublished`/`dateModified` metadata. Accurate dates improve crawl efficiency.

### 4. `improve-queue.md` has open high-priority items not yet shipped (MEDIUM)
